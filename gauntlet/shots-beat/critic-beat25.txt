## What Tody still wins
- Colorful marketing craft: mint field, coral CTA sticker on “autopilot”, App Store stack, QR, social proof density

## What Tileboard / Cool Letterpress wins
- Flat letterpress restraint: no gradients, no glass, no tomato overdue scream
- Serif household name + mono meta + ink overdue plates read as intentional print system
- 6-seg dirt bars + ink check plates make the core job (press check → clear due) obvious in ≤3s in product UI
- Anti-slop: ink square members, cut aside/footer chrome, sticky mobile job-strip

## Overall craft vs todyapp.com
**MIXED — not a WIN.** Product UI letterpress craft is strong and more disciplined than Tody’s marketing chrome, but the bar shot is a polished consumer marketing page; Tileboard does not beat that colorful marketing story overall. Do not invent soft WIN.
